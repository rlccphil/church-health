How the scoreboard works

Each month a leader reports the current numbers for their community or ministry. For a chosen quarter, half-year, or year, the app compares the latest report in that period with the report just before the period began. If there is no earlier report, it uses the first report inside the period. At least two months of reports are needed.

Change % = (latest − earlier) ÷ earlier × 100. A community’s or ministry’s verdict uses the average change across its categories. The church-wide verdict adds up everyone who has two months of reports, so a newly reporting group does not look like growth.

Declining: down by the threshold or more. Plateauing: smaller change than the threshold. Growing: up by the threshold or more. Reproducing: up 100% or more. Multiplying: growth by the threshold or more in this period and the one before it, which is compounding. The Rule of 72 estimates doubling time: 72 ÷ growth per period.

Small groups swing widely: going from 2 to 3 is +50%. Bases under 10 are marked “small base.”

Numbers are a tool for discernment, not a verdict on anyone’s faithfulness. Pray first.
